01 / BATCHES & EXPIRY

The oldest medicine leaves the shelf first — automatically.

Every purchase becomes its own batch, with its own expiry date. When a sale happens, Batchly quietly pulls from whichever batch is closest to expiring — not whichever was bought first. If a batch has already expired, the sale is blocked outright, unless a manager deliberately overrides it, and that override is logged.
